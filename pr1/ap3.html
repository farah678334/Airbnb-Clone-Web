About this space

Unwind at this beachside chalet in a central location of Batroun. Fully furnished for your comfort.
Enjoy a sea view from the terrace, the chalet is located in a central location only few minutes walking distance to the beach and popular beach bars.

Batroun is the ideal leisure destination. It is one of the most beautiful coastal cities in Lebanon, and one of the oldest in the world.
It has history, architecture, churches, beaches, bars, seafood, lemonade, and much more!

A copy of the passport is required upon check-in. Please be advised that the rooftop and the Jacuzzi have no ceiling thus they can't be used during rainy weather.

The space

The Chalet consists of one bedroom with a comfortable living space, one bathroom, a kitchenette and a terrace with a sea view.

- Always check if the gas tank is open when you need to use the stove.
