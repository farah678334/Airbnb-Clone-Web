The space

Palm Island is a newly renovated modern studio, located in the prestigious and central Carré d'Or district, offering an ideal retreat for a pleasant stay in Nice.

With a welcoming and relaxing atmosphere, this charming studio boasts a delightful private terrace overlooking a tranquil garden, providing the perfect setting for moments of outdoor relaxation. Inside, you will find a large and comfortable Murphy queen-size bed (160X200), a 55-inch TV with access to Netflix and other entertainment apps, and a modern bathroom with a spacious shower. The fully equipped kitchen allows you to easily prepare meals during your stay. For fitness enthusiasts, additionally, the apartment provides an extra benefit with the convenience of a club located right below the building.

Situated in a prime location in Nice, Palm Island offers convenient access to the main attractions, restaurants, and beaches in the area. Explore the picturesque Old Town of Nice or relax on the shores of the Mediterranean – everything is easily accessible. Our commitment is to ensure that your stay is as enjoyable as possible, and we are available to provide information on the best restaurants, tourist attractions, and local activities. We are here to assist you in planning your itinerary and to show you the hidden treasures of Nice. We look forward to welcoming you and making your stay an unforgettable experience.
