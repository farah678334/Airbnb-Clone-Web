Apartment Description

About this space

Located in the heart of Milan, Navigli area, right in the center, a few minutes from the main city attractions. From our terrace you can enjoy a breathtaking view of the entire city. You will admire Milan from above from a unique perspective, including the famous Golden Madonna of Milan's CATHEDRAL.

All this will allow you to immerse yourself in the true Milanese spirit and fully experience the splendid capital of Italian fashion and design.

You will live a unique experience in authentic Milan.

The space

The apartment is located on the sixth floor with elevator.

You will stay in a typical Milanese house, consisting of a bedroom with a large double bed with two abat-jour with two usb entrances, to be able to load comfortably from the bed, dated smart working area of Wi-Fi donwload 587.6 Mbit/s - upload 627 Mbit/s.

A modern and equipped kitchen, living area with a comfortable sofa bed (140x200cm), bathroom, with a wonderful view of the Naviglio and the Milanese view.
You can enjoy all this with a good glass of wine among the lights of the sunset sky.

The house has all the necessary accessories: iron and iron, washing machine, linen racks, dishwasher, air conditioning and ventilation and cooling systems, heating, 36"Smart TV, towels and sheets washed at 60°.

Also, if you need, we offer extra laundry service for a fee.

In rainy weather, we provide umbrellas to be able to experience the Milanese charm in this case.

The apartment and condominium are very quiet, ideal for relaxing with your partner, friends and family, having fun thanks to the many clubs in the area.

FREE: Water for each guest, tea corner, Italian coffee pods, basic ingredients for cooking, Netflix.

We provide online guides to guide you to the best places to visit in the city and tips to enjoy the typical local and Italian cuisine.

The staff of Regarde le ciel is very attentive to cleanliness and hospitality, which is why we give each guest a courtesy kit with olive oil (shampoo, soap, shower gel).

From LINATE AIRPORT: (taxi €25)
Take the M4 Metro Line to Tricolore station.
