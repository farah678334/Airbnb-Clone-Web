Apartment Description

About this space

S. Damaso apt is a bright and spacious apartment suitable for families of 4 people (max 4 people), a quiet and elegant setting a few steps from St. Peter's and the city center.
Adjacent to Via Gregorio VII, an area full of shops, restaurants and wine bars, which leads in 1.4 km to Vatican City and already in the first minutes on foot to the historic center, you can pleasantly walk and look at the Dome of St. Peter's.
Served by the San Pietro station 900 meters away, 3 bus stops to the center. Metro 1.2km away.

The space

The apartment at Via S. Damaso 34 is located on the second floor (with an elevator) with a south-facing exposure, very bright and warm, in a prestigious building on a reserved and quiet street in the Aurelio neighborhood.
To access the condominium, you must climb some stairs.
Recently renovated apartment, it consists of a large entrance, from which you can access the living room, comfortable and bright and with a balcony, the kitchen and the hallway that leads to the master bedroom and the bathroom.

The living room is equipped with a dining table for 4 people, a large sofa with a double bed (160x200), a 55" Smart TV, from which you can access the balcony with a small table for an aperitif with a splendid view of the condominium greenery.
The kitchen is fully equipped (large fridge/freezer, oven, dishwasher, microwave, toaster, Nespresso coffee machine, kettle, breakfast table) and a small service balcony for the drying rack, etc.

From the entrance you enter the hallway, which leads to the large master bedroom with a double bed (160x200), a wardrobe and armchairs, and to the spacious bathroom with a window, equipped with a large shower for 2 people, a washer/dryer and a bidet. Hygiene products and basic accessories such as hair dryer, hair straightener, etc.

All rooms have windows and hot/cold ducted air conditioning.

The apartment is equipped with ultra-fast fiber Wi-Fi

The apartment is not rented to groups under the age of 25.

It is located 1.5 km (20 minutes on foot) from the Vatican, St. Peter's Basilica, Castel S, Angelo and the historic center, 900 meters from the S. Pietro Station and 1.2 km from the metro A stop Valle Aurelia and 1 km from Villa Pamphili.

Guest access

The apartment is not inhabited and is fully accessible to guests.

Other things to note

N.B. in case of check-in after 10:30 p.m., an extra €20 will be applied to be paid directly to the person who will check-in. After midnight, the extra is €60.

- The price is for 2 people in the bedroom. You can also decide to use the sofa bed on request, if the guests want to sleep separately, with an extra cost of 15 euros for the additional linen.

Although a final cleaning fee is applied, it is good practice to leave the kitchen tidy, with the dishes washed (for this, the dishwasher and soap tabs are available).
Please also do not leave trash in the house. On the way there are the appropriate containers. Thank you!

Ultra-fast 100% fiber wifi (up to 1Gb/s)
